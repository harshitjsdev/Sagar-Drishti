# Project Overview

## Introduction

SagarDrishti is an AI-assisted, browser-based science education platform
focused on ocean data and interactive learning. It combines an ocean
explorer with observation retrieval, model-data integration, guided
learning and an Ocean Tutor. Its educational focus includes
government-school and Hindi-medium learners.

## Educational context

Students in inland Madhya Pradesh may see the ocean as distant from
daily life. The Indian Ocean is part of the wider climate system
associated with the monsoon, while monsoon rainfall is relevant to
agriculture, water availability and livelihoods in the state.
SagarDrishti uses this connection to make ocean literacy locally
meaningful.

**SagarDrishti is an educational platform, not a monsoon forecast,
farmer advisory, operational ocean-monitoring authority or emergency
guidance service.**

## Problem addressed

Ocean science can be difficult to teach through static diagrams and text
alone. Scientific datasets may require specialist software, terminology
and interpretation. Learners can benefit from a single environment where
they can visualize information, explore measurements, ask questions and
check understanding.

## Objectives

-   Make ocean-science concepts easier to explore visually.
-   Provide access to ocean observations and related model information
    when available.
-   Support guided lessons and quizzes.
-   Offer AI-assisted explanations through the Ocean Tutor when
    configured.
-   Support English/Hindi interaction in the tutor experience.
-   Connect ocean and climate concepts to the Indian
    Ocean--monsoon--rainfall--agriculture context.
-   Clearly distinguish observations, model output and fallback/demo
    content.

## Intended users

**School students:** explore ocean conditions, inspect profiles, follow
lessons and attempt quizzes.\
**Government-school and Hindi-medium learners:** access visual
exploration and language-supported tutoring.\
**Teachers and science educators:** use the explorer and guided content
for demonstrations and inquiry activities.\
**Parents and communities:** use the monsoon connection to discuss
environmental and climate science.

These are intended user groups. Adoption and measurable benefits require
real-world evaluation.

## Solution components

1.  Browser-based interactive ocean explorer.
2.  Backend services for observation retrieval and model sampling.
3.  Guided lessons, quizzes and explanations.
4.  AI-assisted Ocean Tutor.
5.  Data-source, status and reliability handling.

## Current foundation

Project materials describe a 3D browser explorer, Flask backend,
INCOIS/Argo retrieval, Copernicus model sampling, guided lessons,
quizzes and Ocean Tutor integration. External services depend on
configuration, coverage and connectivity.

## Boundaries

Do not assume guaranteed live data for all regions, fully contextual AI
for every selection, adaptive learning, large-scale analytics, validated
learning gains or completed school deployment unless current source and
evaluation evidence confirm them.
