# Education and Intended Impact

## Educational vision

SagarDrishti aims to help students move from memorizing scientific facts
to observing evidence, asking questions, understanding patterns and
applying concepts to their world. It uses real observations, interactive
visualization, guided lessons, quizzes and AI-assisted explanations.

## Relevance to Madhya Pradesh

The educational narrative is:

**Indian Ocean → Monsoon → Rainfall → Agriculture → Local livelihoods**

This helps inland learners understand why ocean science can be relevant
to rainfall, farming and water-related discussions in Madhya Pradesh.
SagarDrishti is educational and is not a monsoon forecasting or farmer
advisory system.

## Intended beneficiaries

-   **School students:** visual exploration, observations, lessons,
    quizzes and explanations.
-   **Government-school learners:** browser-based access and simplified
    content aim to reduce some access barriers.
-   **Hindi-medium learners:** English/Hindi tutor interaction can
    support familiar-language learning.
-   **Teachers and educators:** visual demonstration and
    inquiry-learning resource.
-   **Parents and communities:** context for environmental and climate
    literacy.

These are intended benefits, not measured outcomes.

## Learning cycle

1.  **Observe:** view the 3D ocean, map or profile.
2.  **Explore:** change region, depth, time or variable.
3.  **Question:** ask the Ocean Tutor or use a guided prompt.
4.  **Explain:** read a plain-language explanation.
5.  **Compare:** examine observation and model information where
    available.
6.  **Assess:** attempt a quiz or data-interpretation question.

## Educational value

The project may support conceptual understanding of ocean layers,
temperature, salinity and currents; data literacy through actual
measurements; inquiry through learner questions; and scientific thinking
through comparison of observations with model information.

## Classroom use

Potential uses include teacher-led demonstrations, individual
exploration, group discussions, guided lessons and quiz-supported
activities. Curriculum mapping and teacher activity packs should be
called future work unless verified in the current release.

and measured student outcomes are not available. Future pilots could

## Responsible claims

Until pilot evidence exists, use "designed to support," "aims to improve
accessibility" and "potential benefit." Avoid unsupported percentages,
guaranteed learning gains or claims of nationwide impact.

## Impact statement

SagarDrishti seeks to turn ocean data into understandable science,
understandable science into curiosity, and curiosity into learning
relevant to students' lives in Madhya Pradesh.
