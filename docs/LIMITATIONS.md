# Limitations and Constraints

## External services

INCOIS/Argo, Copernicus Marine and Gemini depend on external
availability, network conditions, credentials where required and service
policies. Live results cannot be guaranteed for every request.

## Data coverage

Argo coverage varies by location, time, depth and variable. An empty
result does not mean that no ocean condition exists; it may mean no
matching observation was returned.

## Model versus observation

Model output is not a direct measurement. Differences can reflect model
representation, resolution, coverage, sampling and processing.
Comparison requires context and careful interpretation.

## AI reliability

AI-generated explanations may be incomplete, unclear or incorrect.
Gemini availability depends on configuration and provider conditions.
Demo fallback is not equivalent to a live AI response and must be
labelled.

## Device and browser variation

3D graphics, touch interaction and browser speech capabilities vary
across devices. Older school hardware and low-bandwidth connections may
affect usability.

## Educational evidence

The impact materials do not provide validated field-study results or
measured student outcomes. Benefits remain intended or potential until
evaluated through a structured pilot.

## Deployment maturity

Production readiness, large-scale performance, monitoring, security
posture and school deployment need verification and ongoing testing.

## Non-goals

SagarDrishti is an educational platform, not an operational monsoon
prediction system, weather service, agricultural advisory or emergency
guidance source.

## Features requiring verification/further work

Curriculum mapping, adaptive learning, AI-generated assessments,
detailed analytics, expanded languages and large-scale school adoption
are described as future work or areas for further development. Confirm
current implementation before claiming availability.
